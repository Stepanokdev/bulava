# Contributing to Bulava

Pull requests are welcome. Before you open one, please read the terms below. Opening a pull request
means you accept them for everything in it.

1. **Your work stays yours.** You keep the copyright in what you contribute.
2. **You licence it to the project.** You grant Ivan Stepanok, the author and maintainer of Bulava,
   a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable licence to use, copy, modify,
   publish, distribute and sublicense your contribution, and to release it under the project's
   current licence (FSL-1.1-ALv2) or any later licence the project adopts, open-source or not.
3. **You have the right to give it.** The contribution is your own work, or you have permission to
   submit it under these terms. If an employer or a client has rights in what you write, for example
   because you wrote it during work hours or for their project, you have their permission to
   contribute it, and you say so in the pull request.
4. **No confidential material.** Nothing in the contribution belongs to an employer, a client or
   anyone else who has not agreed to its publication: no ticket numbers, server names, customer
   names, internal URLs or data.

## Signing off

Add a sign-off line to every commit to confirm the terms above:

```
Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>
```

`git commit -s` adds it for you. Use an address you control personally, not one that belongs to an
employer.

## Practical notes

- One change per pull request, with a short description of what it does and how you checked it.
- Run the engine's tests (`engine/tests/`) and the app's test target if your change touches them.
